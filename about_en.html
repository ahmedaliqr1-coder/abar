About Aber Vehicle Registration Services

Who We Are

Aber Vehicle Registration Services L.L.C is a licensed private commercial company specializing in vehicle-related services, registration, and brokerage. We are officially registered under commercial license number 816358, issued by the Department of Economy and Tourism in Dubai.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide efficient, transparent, and accessible vehicle registration and inquiry services to the public. We aim to simplify complex vehicle-related processes through innovative digital solutions while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and compliance.

Our Vision

We envision becoming the leading digital platform for vehicle services in the region, offering seamless, user-friendly solutions that empower individuals and businesses to manage their vehicle-related needs with confidence and ease.

Core Values

Transparency: We maintain complete transparency in all our operations and clearly disclose our commercial nature and licensing details.

Reliability: We are committed to providing accurate, timely, and dependable services to all our users.

Security: We prioritize the protection and confidentiality of user data through advanced security measures.

Excellence: We continuously strive to improve our services and exceed customer expectations.

Licensed Activities

Under our commercial license, we are authorized to provide the following services:

• Vehicle Services Brokerage

• Vehicle Technical Inspection Services

• Vehicle Registration Services

Why Choose Aber?

Licensed and Regulated: We operate under a valid commercial license and comply with all applicable regulations.

Professional Team: Our experienced staff is dedicated to providing exceptional customer service.

Aber is fully committed to complying with all laws, regulations, and licensing requirements set by the Department of Economy and Tourism and other relevant authorities. We maintain transparency about our commercial nature and regularly update our practices to align with regulatory developments.

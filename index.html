Service Description

This service provided by Aber Vehicle Registration Services L.L.C allows easy inquiry regarding traffic fines registered against vehicles.

Inquiry Steps

  • Inquiry by Vehicle Number: Enter the vehicle number and select the category.
  • Inquiry by Personal Number: Click on the personal number tab and enter your personal ID number.
  • Inquiry by Establishment Number: Click on the establishment number tab and enter the company number.
  • Enter the verification code and click on Inquire.
Fees: There are no fees for this service.
Frequently Asked Questions
1. Are there any financial fees for the traffic fines inquiry service? +
No, the service is completely free of charge and there are no fees for inquiring about traffic fines through our platform.
2. How can I inquire using the vehicle number? +
You can do this by entering the traffic plate number, choosing the category and associated code, then entering the verification code and clicking inquire.
3. Can companies inquire using an establishment number? +
Yes, by clicking on the establishment number tab and entering the company's specific number to view all associated vehicles and fines.
4. What documents are required for personal inquiry? +
The inquiry process only requires entering the approved personal number or accurate identification details to retrieve the traffic record.
5. What should you do if an error occurs in the verification code? +
You must ensure that the numbers displayed in the image are written correctly. If the error persists, you can refresh the page and try again.
6. Does the service cover all emirates of the country? +
The service is dedicated to vehicles and plates registered in accordance with the regulatory scope and approved data for the vehicle services sector.
7. How is the personal number used in the system? +
By switching to the personal number tab in the inquiry interface and then entering the number with its correct digits.
8. Do fines appear immediately and updated? +
Yes, the system displays the latest recorded data associated with the vehicle number or traffic file at the moment of inquiry.
9. Is Aber considered an official government entity? +
No, Aber Vehicle Registration Services is a registered private company and not an official government entity.
10. How can I contact support if there is a technical problem? +
You can contact us via the support channels available on the website or visit our main headquarters in Dubai Industrial City.

Policies and Regulations - Aber Vehicle Registration Services

1. Legislative Framework

Aber Vehicle Registration Services L.L.C is committed to complying with all regulations and commercial legislation issued by official regulatory authorities in the country governing the vehicle services and brokerage sector.

2. Scope of Licensed Activities

The company operates within the boundaries of activities approved under its commercial license number 816358, which includes vehicle services brokerage, technical inspection, goods transportation inspection services, and vehicle registration.

3. Service Quality Standards

The platform is committed to implementing the highest standards of quality and efficiency in providing inquiry services and facilitating digital transactions to ensure a seamless and reliable user experience for all users.

4. Compliance and Transparency Regulations

The platform maintains complete transparency in disclosing commercial license details, activity names, and clarifying the nature of the entity as a private commercial company, not a sovereign government entity.

5. Information Security and Confidentiality Policies

All data processing operations, license plate numbers, and identification information are subject to strict regulations ensuring prevention of unauthorized access or exploitation of information in any manner contrary to law.

6. Inquiry Controls and Tool Usage

Internal regulations define the use of inquiry tools exclusively for their designated purposes only (inquiry by vehicle number, personal ID, or establishment registration number) without any technical violations.

7. Technical Issue Reporting and Resolution

The company has established clear regulations for receiving and resolving any technical problems or operational inquiries faced by visitors through available communication channels and the technical support team.

8. Data Update and Pricing Policy

Management is committed to regularly reviewing and updating platform content and announced fees (if any) to ensure complete compliance with administrative and regulatory updates to the license.

9. Trademark Usage Rules

All logos and trademarks for the name "Aber" are subject to strict intellectual property protection, and it is strictly prohibited for any other entity to reuse or imitate them without formal written authorization.

10. Misuse Prevention Policy

Regulations prohibit the use of programming robots or systematic pressure attempts and server intrusion, and the company reserves the right to take all legal measures against violators.

11. Consumer Protection Compliance

All regulations regarding user rights and protection of their interests are observed when providing brokerage services and facilities related to vehicles through the electronic platform.

12. Review and Amendment of Regulatory Provisions

The company management reserves the right to modify or add new provisions to these regulations in accordance with business requirements and legislative developments, which become effective immediately upon posting here.

13. Cooperation with Official Authorities

The company is fully committed to cooperating with competent official authorities and providing necessary facilities in case of any request for regulatory data in accordance with applicable legal frameworks.

14. User Responsibility and Regulatory Compliance

The platform user bears full responsibility for complying with announced regulations and not exploiting services in any activities that violate the laws in force in the country.

15. Accreditation and Official Documentation

These regulations constitute an internal regulatory document approved to govern the relationship between Aber and platform users, and continued use constitutes full acceptance of these terms.
